N: 82
T: Newer and Older, Odor
S: In a *quaint* old village, the *archive* held *archaic* letters and an *antiquated*, now *obsolete* printing press. A young student of *archeology* loved the place, though her own taste was *contemporary* and she stayed *au courant*. An *outmoded* machine and a *superannuated* clerk worked beside an *obsolescent* stove. In the garden, *fragrant* roses grew beside a *fetid* pond that would *reek* in summer, and *aromatic* tea made the *rancid* smell easier to forget. A *malodorous* drain and a *noisome* ditch were fixed, and the *olfactory* joy returned.
