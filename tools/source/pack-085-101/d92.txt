T: Quarrel
S: Two brothers would *quarrel* daily, and their *belligerent* mood filled the house. A silly *squabble* grew into a *brawl*, and their uncle rushed in to *quell* the *fracas*. One tried to *wrest* the toy away, and the other began to *pummel* him. Seeing the *estranged* pair full of *hostility*, Mother feared *revenge* would *alienate* them for years. She asked for a *candid* talk. Soon the once *contentious* boys turned *amiable*, even *affable*, and chose to *grapple* only in a friendly wrestling game.
