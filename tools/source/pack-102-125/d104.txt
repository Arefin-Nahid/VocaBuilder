N: 104
T: Secrets
S: A *clandestine* club met in an old cellar, and its *arcane* rituals were *recondite* to outsiders. Members would *skulk* through the lanes with *furtive* glances and *lurk* near the gate to *waylay* spies. They would *secrete* a *cache* of old maps and write notes in *cipher*. A *cryptic* message and an *enigmatic* smile were all a newcomer got.
