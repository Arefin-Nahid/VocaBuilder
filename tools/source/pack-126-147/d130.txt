# Putting into words
verbatim|adj|word for word|অক্ষরে অক্ষরে|He repeated the message *verbatim*.
verbalize|v|put into words|ভাষায় প্রকাশ করা|Love can't be entirely *verbalized*.
liaise|v|communicate and maintain contact|যোগাযোগ রক্ষা করা|He will *liaise* with our seniors about the plan.
euphemism|n|an inoffensive or indirect expression substituted for one considered offensive or too harsh|শ্রুতিকটু শব্দের পরিবর্তে কোমল শব্দের প্রয়োগ|“Passed away” is a *euphemism* for “died”.
understatement|n|a restrained statement instead of explicitly saying it|কটুভাষায় না বলে একটু হাল্কা করে বলা|“War is unpleasant” is an *understatement*.
# Figures of speech
rhetoric|n|the art of effective communication; insincere or grandiloquent language|ভাষার অলংকরণ|The candidate's *rhetoric* stirred the crowd.
figurative|adj|metaphorical; not literal; using figures of speech|আক্ষরিক নয়, প্রতীকী অর্থযুক্ত|“Heart of stone” is *figurative* language.
trope|n|a figure of speech using words in a nonliteral way|বক্রোক্তি; প্রতীকী অর্থে বলা|The hero's sacrifice is a common *trope* in stories.
metaphor|n|a figure of speech that compares two different things|রূপক|“He is a lion in battle” is a *metaphor*.
simile|n|a figure of speech that compares things of different kinds, usually with “like” or “as”|উপমা|“He fights like a lion” is a *simile*.
idiom|n|an expression whose meaning as a whole differs from the meaning of its individual words|বাগধারা|“Lose one's marbles” is a common *idiom*.
oxymoron|n|conjoining contradictory terms|বিপরীত জিনিসকে একত্র করা|“Deafening silence” is an *oxymoron*.
